## Findings, most severe first

There is no High. No item breaks an app that installs it: both smoke apps take the six items in one `add`, pull their 13 dependencies, build a prerendered page composing the provider, the sidebar, a menu, a dialog, the log and the composer, type-check with 0 errors, pass our lint, and a second `add` is byte-identical (EXECUTED).

1. **Medium — The log stops following after a gesture that moves nothing: a wheel towards the end, or End, ArrowDown, PageDown or Space while already at the end** (`src/lib/registry/ui/message-log/follow-bottom.svelte.ts:53-60`; `message-log.svelte:79-90`; decision 11).
   Failure: `release()` sets `following = false` on any `wheel`, any `touchmove` and seven keys, with no regard to direction or position. At the end of the box these gestures produce no `scroll` event, so nothing re-arms the follow; the next growth then leaves the box behind. EXECUTED (`probe2.ts`, the main log on `/items/message-log/`): at the end (`scrollTop` 244, `atEnd` true), `mouse.wheel(0, +120)` → still 244 and `atEnd` true, the button hidden; "Stream a reply" → mid-stream and after, `scrollTop` 244, `atEnd` **false**, the button **shown**. Then, back at the end, End on the focused region → `scrollTop` 351, `atEnd` true; "Add a turn" → 351, `atEnd` **false**. A reader who flicks the wheel down to be sure they are at the bottom, or presses End, has not "scrolled away" (decision 11's rule), yet the pane stops following for the rest of the reply. L1 and behaviour 9 release by a wheel up and ArrowUp, so neither sees it.
   Fix: release only on a gesture that can move the box away from its end: `onwheel` when `event.deltaY < 0`; the keys `ArrowUp`, `PageUp` and `Home` (the other four cannot leave the end, and when they do scroll, the `scroll` handler's fall rule and the 8 px re-arm already apply); `touchmove` the same way, or drop it and let the fall rule catch a touch scroll. An equivalent guard with less reasoning: `release()` measures first and returns while `atEnd` (a real move up still releases through the fall rule a frame later). Add to L1: a wheel of +120 at the end, then a stream, keeps `atEnd` and the button hidden; and to behaviour 9: End while at the end, then "Add a turn", is followed. Amend decision 11's sentence to "a gesture that moves the box away from its end", and record the call.

2. **Medium — A top or bottom sheet has no height cap, so a long body runs past the viewport and does not scroll inside** (`src/lib/registry/ui/sheet/sheet-content.svelte:50`; decision 8).
   Failure: the side classes are `data-[side=bottom]:inset-x-0 data-[side=bottom]:bottom-0 data-[side=bottom]:h-auto` (and the mirror for `top`); `overflow-y-auto scrollbar` is on the root, but with `h-auto` and no `max-height` there is nothing to overflow. EXECUTED (`probe.ts`, the bottom sheet on `/items/sheet/` given a 1,600 px child): the panel measures 1,917 px tall with its top at −1,017 px, `max-height: none`, `scrollHeight` 1,917 = `clientHeight` 1,917. The title, the description and the 32 px close button (`absolute top-4`) are above the viewport and cannot be scrolled to; Escape and the scrim still close it. The side sheets are safe through `h-full`. Decision 8 says a long body scrolls inside "for the same reason" as the dialog; the page says so too. An app's bottom sheet with a form taller than a phone's viewport is the case.
   Fix: `data-[side=top]:max-h-full data-[side=bottom]:max-h-full` (or `max-h-full` on the root; `h-full` on the sides is unaffected). In N2's four-sides test, give the bottom specimen a long body (or inject one) and assert `max-height` is `100%`, the box lies inside the viewport and `scrollHeight > clientHeight`.

3. **Low — The dialog's close button is positioned by bits-ui's inline style, not by ours** (`src/lib/registry/ui/dialog/dialog-content.svelte:45`, `:64`).
