Kuiper high contrast: the swatches

Non-text (inputs, borders, focus, the glass edge): 4.5:1. Grounds, the marks and the brand fills that already pass do not change. Ratios as scripts/contrast.ts computes them: WCAG 2.2, truncated to two decimals. Struck figures are the source colour's.
